TECHNICAL DECISIONS AND WHY
----------------------------
- Go 1.25 instead of 1.26: Gin requires 1.25 and that is what is installed.
  Update go.mod to 1.26 when it becomes available.
- FastTrack patterns carried over: per-domain DB files, struct-based config,
  validation with field names in error messages, handwritten mocks, CLAUDE.md
  hierarchy, getWithCache[T any] generics, idempotent migrations.
- FastTrack patterns NOT carried over: gRPC, GraphQL, Kafka, ClickHouse, Redis
  (too heavy for this project).
- workout.Service uses interfaces (Storage, UserStorage) instead of concrete types
  — testable without a real DB. This paid off immediately when writing tests.
- XP stored on the workout row at generation time (xp_earned column), not
  recalculated at completion. Makes the reward predictable and unaffected by
  future formula changes.
- CompleteAndAwardXP is on WorkoutStore (not a separate Transactor interface)
  because the transaction touches both tables and belongs in the data layer.
  The service interface hides this from the business logic layer.
- Gemini strips markdown fences defensively — the model ignores the JSON-only
  instruction in the system prompt sometimes.
